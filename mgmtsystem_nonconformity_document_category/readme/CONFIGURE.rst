At the installation, the OCA category *Procedure* (module
``document_page_procedure``) gets the type *Procedure*, whatever it is called.
The other categories are classified by hand, as described in
``mgmtsystem_document_category``: the OCA categories *Environmental Aspect*,
*Quality Manual* and *Environment Manual* when their modules are installed,
and the categories created in the database.

A category classified while a nonconformity is open is offered at the next
search, without reloading the page.
