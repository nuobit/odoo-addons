On a Spanish point of sale, ``l10n_es_pos`` prints the customer's name,
tax ID and address on the ticket of every order that has a customer
other than its generic one, simplified invoices included. A simplified
invoice carries the buyer's tax ID and address only when the buyer is a
business or a professional who asks for them, or needs them for a tax
right (Real Decreto 1619/2012, article 7.2 and 7.3).

With this module, the ticket of a simplified invoice prints the customer
only when the customer has a tax ID. The ticket of an invoice is
unchanged.

Known issues / Roadmap
======================

- On Odoo 17.0, ``l10n_es_pos`` loses the simplified invoice of an order
  whose ticket is rebuilt from the server's data (reprinted from the
  orders list, or printed after an online payment), and makes a full
  invoice of one validated again after a refusal. Their tickets print
  the customer like a regular one.
