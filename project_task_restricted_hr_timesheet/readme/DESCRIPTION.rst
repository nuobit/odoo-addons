This module hides the timesheet lines of the tasks marked **Restricted** from
everyone outside the **Restricted tasks** group, managers and timesheet
approvers included: in every list, report and RPC call, and on the customer
portal, in its timesheet list and in the hours a task's page counts for its
subtasks. Members keep the timesheet access they already have. The lines of
normal tasks, and the analytic lines without a task, are left as they are.

It completes *Project Task Restricted* where Timesheets is installed, and
installs itself with both.
