On a customer invoice, a customer credit note or a sales receipt, printed or shown
on the customer portal, the description column prints the name of each line's
product under the header "Product": without the product's internal reference and
without the rest of the line's label. A line without a product prints its own label.
A product line whose label is empty prints nothing, as in Odoo. What other modules
add to the column still prints, except in the cases listed under Known issues /
Roadmap. Vendor bills, vendor credit notes and purchase receipts keep Odoo's own
column.
