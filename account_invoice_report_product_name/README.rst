On a customer invoice, a customer credit note or a sales receipt,
printed or shown on the customer portal, the description column prints
the name of each line's product under the header "Product": without the
product's internal reference and without the rest of the line's label. A
line without a product prints its own label. A product line whose label
is empty prints nothing, as in Odoo. What other modules add to the
column still prints, except in the cases listed under Known issues /
Roadmap. Vendor bills, vendor credit notes and purchase receipts keep
Odoo's own column.

Known issues / Roadmap
======================

- A product variant prints only the name of its product, without its
  attributes.
- The cell reads the product's current name every time the invoice is
  printed: an invoice printed again after its product is renamed shows
  the new name.
- The rest of the line's label is not printed: the lines typed into it
  and the product's sales description.
- A view that finds the span of the line's label by its options no
  longer finds it, because this module changes them. A module with such
  a view and this one cannot both be installed: the one installed second
  fails. Odoo refuses such a view created in the database later, but
  does not check one created there before this module is installed or
  updated: printing an invoice then fails.
- A view applied after this module's that replaces the span of the
  line's label prints its own text instead of the product's name. One
  that replaces the span's options, or turns it into a t-out without
  them, prints the line's label.
- This module's view has priority 1, so that it applies before the
  report's views of a higher priority, Odoo's default of 16 among them.
  A view with a priority below 1, or with 1 and created before it,
  applies first: a span it puts ahead of the header's span prints
  "Product" instead of its own text, the options it gives the span of
  the line's label give way to this module's, and if it removes that
  span, this module's view no longer finds it.
- On a customer document, a product line whose own label has more than
  31 lines still keeps the header of the table from repeating on each
  page: Odoo decides it from every line's label, printed or not.
- A view applied after this module's that replaces Odoo's span of the
  header prints its own text instead of "Product", and what a view adds
  inside that span does not print on a customer document, where the span
  prints "Product" instead.
- A company of a Gulf country prints its invoices with the Arabic and
  English template of ``l10n_gcc_invoice``, which this module leaves as
  it is.
