Odoo prints "PROFORMA" before the title of a posted invoice when it renders
the PDF while the invoice has no official PDF yet: the portal preview, its
print and download buttons, and the PDF that Send & Print attaches when an
electronic document fails.

With this module a posted invoice or credit note never shows "PROFORMA", and
that attached PDF takes the name of the official one. Draft and cancelled
invoices, including those reset to draft, keep Odoo's behaviour.
