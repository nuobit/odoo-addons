The module adds the Administrator user to the group, and adds it again on every
update. After installing it, add the other users who must see restricted tasks,
before any task is marked: open the user in *Settings > Users & Companies >
Users* and tick **Restricted tasks** in the *Other* section of the *Access
Rights* tab. The group implies *Internal User*: portal and public users cannot
join it.

To take someone out of the group, untick the same box, or change their *User
Type* to portal or public, or remove them from the *Users* tab of the group.
The *User Type* and the menu *Settings > Users & Companies > Groups* show only
in developer mode. Each of these forms warns, before saving, how many
restricted tasks they will be taken off. If they still have activities on
restricted tasks, assign those activities to a member or mark them as done
first: until then, saving is refused.
