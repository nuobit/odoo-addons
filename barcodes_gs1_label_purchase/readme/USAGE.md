1. Tick *Dynamic Ratio* on the purchase unit, for instance *Crate*, and give it
   the quantity it usually holds as its ratio: a receipt expects that estimate.
2. Buy in that unit.
3. On the receipt, enter the quantity received and the *Purchase Units
   Received*, which the line proposes: the units of the purchase line still to
   come. A partial receipt leaves the rest on its backorder, which proposes the
   units still to come once the receipt is done.
4. Print the receipt's labels: each unit received gets a label with its share
   of the quantity received.

A receipt done before this module was installed has no purchase units
received: unlock it (*Lock/Unlock*) and enter them before printing its labels.
