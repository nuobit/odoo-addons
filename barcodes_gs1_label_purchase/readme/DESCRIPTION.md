This module prints the receipt labels of goods bought in a unit of measure
whose quantity is known only when the goods are received, such as a crate of
produce that is weighed on arrival. The *Dynamic Ratio* checkbox on the unit's
form marks such a unit.

Each receipt line in such a unit records its *Purchase Units Received*: how
many of the units bought it brings. Each of them gets its own label, which
carries its share of the quantity the receipt received.
